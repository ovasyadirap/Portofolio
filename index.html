About Me

Bridging business strategy and technical execution

I'm a Senior Systems Analyst with over fifteen years of experience helping enterprises rethink how their technology supports the business. My work sits at the intersection of systems architecture, process engineering, and data strategy — I audit legacy environments, model future-state architectures, and lead cross-functional teams through implementation without disrupting day-to-day operations.

I've led enterprise resource planning rollouts, cloud migrations for regulated industries, and automation initiatives that eliminated thousands of hours of manual work annually. I care about systems that are not just functional, but observable, secure, and built to outlast the person who built them.

  • Requirements gathering & stakeholder alignment across C-suite to end users
  • Enterprise architecture design & legacy system modernization
  • Data governance, migration integrity & regulatory compliance (SOC 2, HIPAA, ISO 27001)
  • Process automation & workflow optimization (RPA, scripting, integrations)
